About the portal

The Student Portal is the front end to the academic records the institute already keeps about you. It does not create new paperwork — it just makes the existing records readable, current, and available on a phone at 9 in the morning.

Courses & timetable

Your registered courses for the running semester, with slot, venue, instructor and credit count. The weekly grid highlights the class you are in right now.

Attendance

Per-course percentages updated after each lecture, plus how many sessions you can still miss before you drop under the 75% requirement.

Grades & transcripts

Quiz, mid-semester and end-semester marks as they are released, running SGPA and CGPA, and a printable statement of grades for past semesters.

Profile & settings

Keep your contact number, hostel room and emergency contact accurate, manage your password, and choose which notifications reach your email.

Who can sign in

Any currently enrolled student with an active institute account. Alumni keep read-only access to transcripts for two years after graduation. Faculty and staff use the separate administration console, not this portal.
